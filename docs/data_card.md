# TraceMind synthetic dataset data card

Status: **GENERATION IN PROGRESS**. Twenty individualized scene plans have passed independent, digest-bound model review. Actual dialogue and experiment completion counts are reported separately in `status.json`. No human ratings have been collected. The characters and circumstances are fictional adults, not clinical records. The plans do not establish diagnoses or require positive outcomes.

Active synthesis uses `gemini_v1`: separate stateless API requests with the designated `gemini-3.8-flash` model. The user has approved the exact returned-name mapping to `gemini-3.8-flash-tiered`; both names and the mapping basis are saved. After the first 20-record batch, the user removed that cap and the daily cap, and authorized continued synthesis with at least 10 seconds globally between starts and concurrency up to four. Shared openings remain separately counted. The inherited scene plans retain their original Terra authorship and independent model-review provenance; they are not relabeled as Gemini-authored plans.

The retained v2 collection used a fresh dedicated agent per trajectory-role and shared opening. Its existing dialogues are kept separately and generation is disabled. Earlier v1 shared-role workers retained other-case or future-anchor context, so those engineering samples are excluded from formal counts. No old dialogue is mixed into Gemini generation counts.

The intended design contains 20 groups, four in each of five themes. Within each theme, two groups are development, one calibration, and one test. Each group has four alternative trajectories (stable, gradual, fluctuating, contextual), six planned sessions per trajectory, and three shared checkpoint prompts at sessions 2, 4, and 6. Checkpoint text is designed to fit all four alternative histories; it does not by itself resolve the historical interpretation. The plans are prompts for later authoring, not completed dialogues or validated evidence of quality.

Early scaffold drafts were replaced by individually authored session experiences and disclosure constraints before approval. The reviewer rejected and required repairs for several factual and counterfactual inconsistencies; duplicate-string checks alone did not constitute quality approval. No experiment scores or human judgments are supplied.

At S2/S4/S6, the shared current fragment is the opening client utterance, generated once from the common situation and reused across four histories with its source job retained. Each remaining utterance is separately authored by an isolated client or counselor role. The counselor receives selected public dialogue only. The client receives its profile and the most recent two already-experienced event cards. Public context uses up to five historical sessions' first/last client utterances and the current opening plus most recent eight turns. Actual messages and this fixed selection policy are saved; this is not full-history generation. Reused anchors are not represented as four independent generations.

Private plans, reviews and held-out Gemini data remain under ignored `.local/datasets/gemini_v1/`; `.local/datasets/v2/` retains the earlier Terra collection. Gemini request attempts, actual messages, original responses and batch states remain under ignored `.local/api/`. Provider-reported usage is preserved as returned, not converted into unverified fees. Queue provenance records model names, execution IDs and input/result hashes. Proxy routing and model labels are observable records, not independent proof of the underlying provider model's physical identity. Automated structural checks do not replace dialogue-content or human review.

Rejected-session revisions retain the original dialogue, model rejection and source jobs in an ignored repair archive. A user-authorized pre-freeze revision can replace a suffix in development or calibration data, using new role-isolated task hashes and another independent whole-session review. Shared checkpoint openings and frozen public checkpoint hashes are preserved. Test sessions and all sessions after protocol freeze are excluded from this repair path. A rejected revision stops again; revisioning is not an unlimited retry policy or an approval override.


Rejected sessions now use [bounded independent adjudication and trajectory quarantine](review_flow.md). Original test content and rejected judgments remain unchanged; unresolved sources are excluded while independent trajectories continue.
